Privacy notice

Last updated 25 October 2026

This notice covers the map at amex-explorer.kooexperience.com, the Table for Two email reminders and the Table for Two Telegram bot. It describes what each part collects and why. It is written to the standard of Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA).

The service is run by Koo Haoming (individual, Singapore). It is an unofficial fan site and is not affiliated with American Express. For any privacy question or request, email privacy@kooexperience.com.

1. Browsing the map

You can use the map without an account. The site sets no cookies and does not ask for your location. It stores three small preferences in your browser's local storage:

Services your browser contacts

Loading the map makes your browser request files from the services below. Each one receives your IP address and browser details as part of the request. The site sends no referrer header to them.

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site files.
  • Cloudflare sits in front of the site as a proxy and CDN. Cloudflare Web Analytics: cookieless, aggregate page views, no input capture.
  • unpkg.com serves the Leaflet map library.
  • OpenStreetMap Germany (tile.openstreetmap.de) serves the map tiles. It sees which map area you are viewing.
  • DiningCity (static-assets.diningcity.asia) serves some Table for Two venue photos.
  • The reminders service on Railway serves live Table for Two slot data.

Links to Google Maps, Tabelog, Pocket Concierge, American Express and Telegram only open when you click them. Those sites then apply their own privacy policies.

2. Table for Two email alerts

What is collected

  • Your email address and, if you give it, your name.
  • Your alert choices: party size, venues, sessions, date range and any specific dates.
  • A keyed hash of your IP address, used only to limit repeated sign-ups. It is cleared when you confirm.
  • Sign-up, confirmation and unsubscribe times, plus random tokens for your confirm, manage and unsubscribe links.

Why

To confirm your sign-up, check the latest observed Table for Two slots against your choices and email you when one matches. The rate-limit hash prevents abuse. Your details are not used for marketing or sold.

Consent and withdrawal

No alerts are sent until you click the link in the confirmation email. Every email has a Manage link and an Unsubscribe link that work without a login. Mail apps that support one-click unsubscribe can also stop the emails directly.

How long it is kept

  • Unconfirmed sign-ups are deleted when the confirmation link expires.
  • Unsubscribed records are deleted 30 days after you unsubscribe.
  • Alerts whose date range ended more than 30 days ago are deleted.
  • Rate-limit records are deleted after 24 hours.
  • To avoid sending the same alert twice, the alert job keeps a salted one-way hash of each alert it has sent. This hash contains no readable email address. It is stored in the project's public GitHub repository and is not deleted. [LAWYER REVIEW]

3. Table for Two Telegram bot

  • Your Telegram user ID is never stored as-is. It is turned into a keyed hash, which is used for rate limits and to link your reminders to you.
  • Your chat ID is stored only when you confirm a reminder, so the bot can message you. It is cleared once the reminder is sent, cancelled or past its dates.
  • While you set up a reminder the bot keeps your venue, party size, meal and dates. Unfinished setups expire after 15 minutes.
  • Rate-limit records are deleted after 24 hours. Finished reminders are deleted after 30 days, or 90 days if the last delivery status is unclear.
  • Telegram's update IDs are kept for 7 days so a message is not answered twice. They are not linked to your user ID.
  • The bot does not store the text of your messages. Service logs record the command type and outcome only.

Send /delete_me and reply DELETE to erase all of your Telegram reminder data at once. This removes reminders, setup state, stored chat IDs, delivery receipts and rate-limit records. Telegram keeps its own copy of your chat under its own policy.
